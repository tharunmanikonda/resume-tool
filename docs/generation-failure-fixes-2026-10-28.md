# Generation failure fixes — October 28, 2026

Inspected the seven current extension drafts, their saved generation checkpoints, and local API traces. No live OpenAI requests were made for this investigation or verification.

## Observed failures

- **HuntingCube:** the plan put an explicitly stated DevOps bonus in required, and three qualifications in optional despite their required section context.
- **Atlassian:** two engineering duties were incorrectly optional.
- **Vynca:** three implementation responsibilities were incorrectly optional.
- **VibeIQ:** the checker interpreted a preference for TypeScript/Node.js as downgrading the surrounding required backend experience. The parser also did not recognize Required Experience and Essential Functions headings.
- **C4Y:** 17 generated bullets violated the 25–30-word policy. The bounded repair then copied two old sentences incorrectly (“DB” instead of “database”, and omitted “resilient”), so the exact-match merge rejected the entire patch.
- **SWAKIO:** original writing finished, but review replacements of 24 and 22 words failed local validation. Its original Trigent role also had two numerical bullets out of three, which would have hit an inconsistent extra PDF gate.
- **Arrowmac:** finished with approved review; no failure to fix.

## Changes

1. Resolve clear required/optional classifications from the exact cited JD evidence before deciding whether planning needs a model retry. Record the moves in checkpoint `priority_corrections`. Do not edit project stories or silently accept missing evidence or ambiguous mixed-section citations.
2. Recognize Required Experience, Essential Functions, and Your experience and qualifications headings. Distinguish required experience from a preferred named implementation variant.
3. Preserve completed plans from the previous parser version for matching JD/mode/profile inputs. Re-ground their quotes and rerun targeting instead of discarding the plan and making another full planning request. A changed JD or mode still invalidates incompatible saved work.
4. Add the 25–30-word requirement to the structured bullet schema for writing, bounded repair and coordinated review. Local validation remains in place.
5. Bind repair schema branches to the exact role, bullet position, old sentence and contribution number. Bind headline and skill-group preconditions as well. Shared measurement definitions limit schema repetition. Exact-match, atomic local application remains unchanged.
6. Give the repair call the explicit target table already used by review.
7. Keep numerical coverage as a writing/review goal and remove the contradictory extra numerical hard gate at PDF export. Final word-count and content validation remain active. Do not manufacture measurements to satisfy export.

OpenAI documents `pattern`, `enum`, `$defs` and `anyOf` as supported structured-output features: [Structured Outputs](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/structured-outputs). This is a schema safeguard plus local validation, not a claim that semantic writing quality is guaranteed.
